I am a database guy from North Carolina USA. This site lists my vibecoded projects.

  • Classical Concert Map

    The only interactive map and database of upcoming and historical classical music concerts, filterable by composer and piece. The basis of the project is that thousands of classical music presenters post detailed information about their concerts on their websites, and they're eager for folks to see them--but before Classical Concert Map, no one centralized that information! With the advent of GPT3, it became possible to extract program details from any concert page without custom logic; a few years later, the project now covers tens of thousands of composers and can ingest data from arbitrary historical sources.

  • NC BBQ Map github

    A map of the few remaining old-school North Carolina bbq restaurants.

  • Where Does My Tax Money Go?

    Some folks seem to have no clue whatsoever where their tax money goes; this site breaks down 2025 federal spending by category.

  • Ireland Travel Map

    An extensive interactive map of the sorts of places I like to visit in Ireland and wish I had known about before my first trip.

  • GAA Map github

    A map of hurling and Irish football matches, organized by competition tier.

  • Tennis Map github

    Pro tennis tournaments, mapped, filterable by date and competition tier.

  • Libretto Finder github

    To get into opera, I always enjoy listening while reading the libretto in the original language next to its English translation. This site lists where to find these, organized by composer and opera.

  • Insightful Essays github

    Some writing is just abundantly viscerally insightful, and I wish everyone would read it, regardless of topic.

  • Headline Search github

    GDELT is an extraordinary open-source project that extracts event data from hundreds of millions of news articles. Since 2019, its free data dumps have included article titles; this headline-searcher is a simple interface for quickly searching those article titles.
